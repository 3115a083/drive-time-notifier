# Service images

Small bundled images identify the configured service without runtime image requests.
Brand names and marks identify third-party services, without implying endorsement.

- TomTom, OSRM, GraphHopper, Google Maps and HERE: website favicons, retrieved 2026-11-12 through Google's favicon endpoint for tomtom.com, project-osrm.org, graphhopper.com, maps.google.com and here.com.
- Valhalla: upstream docs/docs/images/valhalla_logo.png in https://github.com/valhalla/valhalla .
- openrouteservice: upstream docs/public/ors_fav.png in https://github.com/GIScience/openrouteservice .
- Photon and Overpass: original app-local search/map pictograms, MIT, rather than invented official logos.

Service images are presentation only. Endpoint hostnames and test results remain visible.
