# Service images

Small bundled images identify the configured service without runtime image requests.
Brand names and marks identify third-party services, without implying endorsement.

- TomTom, GraphHopper and Google Maps: website favicons, retrieved 2026-11-12 through Google's favicon endpoint for tomtom.com, graphhopper.com and maps.google.com.
- Valhalla: upstream docs/docs/images/valhalla_logo.png in https://github.com/valhalla/valhalla .
- openrouteservice: full square symbol from upstream docs/public/ors_fav.png in https://github.com/GIScience/openrouteservice . No cropping of wordmarks.
- OSRM: vector emblem path from images/osrm_logo.svg in https://github.com/Project-OSRM/project-osrm.github.com .
- HERE: official solid monochrome vector, icons/core-ui/SVG/HERE-logo_solid_24px.svg in https://github.com/heremaps/here-icons . Uses the theme foreground for light/dark contrast, without a white background.
- Valhalla's square source is clipped to a circle at render time. All nine service images are checked in light and dark Android screenshots.
- Photon and Overpass: original app-local search/map pictograms, MIT, rather than invented official logos.

Service images are presentation only. Endpoint hostnames and test results remain visible.

HERE solid vector attribution: HERE Technologies, HERE Icons, https://github.com/heremaps/here-icons . Adapted from the 24px SVG into an Android vector drawable with theme-dependent monochrome color. This separate asset is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0; see [license](third-party/HERE-icons-LICENSE.txt).
